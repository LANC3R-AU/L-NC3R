01 / THE CAR

WHERE MY
JOURNEY STARTED.

This 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ is the main project behind L@NC3R.

It's powered by Mitsubishi's naturally aspirated 2.0-litre 4B11 four-cylinder MIVEC engine and paired with a continuously variable transmission.

I'm using the car to learn how automotive systems work rather than simply fitting parts. That means understanding diagnostics, ECU communication, calibration data, maintenance and the effect modifications have on the vehicle.

L@NC3R documents that process from a beginner's perspective — including progress, problems, research and lessons learned along the way.
