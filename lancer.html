01 / OVERVIEW

WHERE MY
JOURNEY STARTED.

This 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer is the main vehicle behind the L@NC3R automotive portfolio.

Rather than treating the car as a collection of random modifications, I'm using it as a platform for learning how the different vehicle systems work together.

That includes the 4B11 engine, Mitsubishi engine management, OBD-II diagnostics, mechanical systems, CVT drivetrain, chassis and future modifications.

The goal is to document what I actually learn and complete as the project develops.
