01 / THE CAR

WHERE MY
JOURNEY STARTED.

This 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ is the main vehicle behind the L@NC3R project.

It's powered by Mitsubishi's naturally aspirated 2.0-litre 4B11 four-cylinder MIVEC engine and paired with a continuously variable transmission.

I'm using the car to understand how automotive systems actually work — from diagnostics and electronic control systems to mechanical components and modifications.

This project records what I learn, what I change, problems I encounter and the progress I make.
