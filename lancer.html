01 / OVERVIEW

MORE THAN
JUST A CAR.

My 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer is the main vehicle behind the L@NC3R project.

Rather than only modifying the car, I'm using it as a platform to understand how automotive systems actually work — from diagnostics and electronic control systems to mechanical components and performance modifications.

This page documents what I learn, what I change, problems I encounter and the progress I make.

I'm still a beginner, so this isn't presented as professional tuning advice. It's a record of my own learning journey and projects.
