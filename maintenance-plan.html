MAINTENANCE DATA NOTICE

The current odometer does not by itself establish whether maintenance is overdue. Previous service history is incomplete, so items remain marked for inspection until a reliable baseline is established. Kilometre calculations are only displayed when a specific target odometer has been recorded.
