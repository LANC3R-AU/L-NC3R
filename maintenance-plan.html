MAINTENANCE DATA NOTICE

The current odometer does not by itself establish whether maintenance is overdue. Previous service history is incomplete. Physical inspection results are recorded separately from completed maintenance, and kilometre calculations are only displayed when a specific target odometer has been recorded.
