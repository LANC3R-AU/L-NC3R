01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

PERFORMANCE
WITH A PURPOSE.

Project 004 is where I'll document the performance side of the Lancer build.

Rather than buying parts without understanding what they change, I want to build the car in stages and understand the reason behind each modification.

That means establishing a stock baseline, understanding the 4B11 engine, collecting data, researching modifications and measuring results whenever possible.

The project currently begins with the factory naturally aspirated 4B11 configuration. Future modifications will be added here only when they're actually planned, installed or tested.
