01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

LEARN BY
DOING.

Project 005 documents the hands-on mechanical side of my automotive journey.

Learning diagnostics and ECU tuning is useful, but I also want to understand the physical systems those electronics are controlling.

This project will document maintenance, inspections, servicing, repairs and mechanical work as I gain more practical experience.

I'm starting as a beginner, so this page is designed to grow alongside my actual experience rather than pretending I've already mastered these systems.

05 / WORKFLOW

WORK
METHODICALLY.

I'm developing a repeatable process for mechanical work instead of jumping straight into removing parts.

01

RESEARCH

Understand the system and correct procedure before beginning work.

02

INSPECT

Check the existing condition and identify anything unusual.

03

PREPARE

Make sure the correct tools, parts and information are available.

04

COMPLETE THE WORK

Follow the correct procedure rather than rushing through the job.

05

CHECK

Inspect the completed work before operating the vehicle.

06

DOCUMENT

Record what was done, what was learned and what needs attention next.
