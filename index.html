WHO I AM

LEARNING CARS
ONE PROJECT AT A TIME.

I'm L@NC3R, a beginner automotive tuner and modder from Australia.

I created this portfolio to document my progression as I learn automotive diagnostics, ECU tuning, modifications and hands-on mechanical work.

I'm not presenting myself as a professional tuner. This site documents the learning process from the beginning — including research, progress, problems and mistakes along the way.

My current main project is my 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ. It's becoming my learning platform for diagnostics, ECU calibration and automotive modification.
