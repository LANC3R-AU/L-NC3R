01 / ABOUT
THE PORTFOLIO
L@NC3R

LEARNING BY
BUILDING.

L@NC3R is my personal automotive portfolio documenting what I learn as I work on cars.

I'm a beginner automotive tuner and modder from Australia. I'm not presenting myself as a professional tuner or mechanic.

Instead, this site records the real process: researching systems, learning diagnostics, understanding ECU calibration, developing mechanical skills, planning modifications and documenting the results.

My main learning platform is my 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ with the 2.0L 4B11 MIVEC engine.

07 / DEVELOPMENT PATH

THE
PROCESS.

The approach behind the L@NC3R project.

01

UNDERSTAND

Learn how the vehicle and its systems operate before changing them.

02

MAINTAIN

Build a healthy mechanical foundation through proper maintenance.

03

DIAGNOSE

Investigate problems using evidence instead of guessing.

04

MEASURE

Use vehicle data and testing to establish what is actually happening.

05

MODIFY

Make researched changes with a clear reason behind them.

06

VALIDATE

Compare results and document what was learned.
