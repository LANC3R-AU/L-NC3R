WHO I AM

LEARNING CARS
ONE PROJECT AT A TIME.

I'm L@NC3R, a beginner automotive tuner and modder from Australia.

I created this portfolio to document my progression as I learn automotive diagnostics, ECU tuning, modifications and hands-on mechanical work.

I'm not presenting myself as a professional tuner. This site is about showing the learning process from the beginning — including the successes, mistakes, research and problems along the way.

My current main project is my 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ, which I'm using to learn more about diagnostics, ECU calibration and vehicle modification.
