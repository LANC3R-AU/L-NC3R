L@NC3R

WHO I AM

LEARNING CARS
ONE PROJECT AT A TIME.

I'm L@NC3R, a beginner automotive tuner and modder from Australia.

This portfolio documents my progression as I learn automotive diagnostics, ECU tuning, performance development, mechanical work and vehicle modification.

I'm not presenting myself as a professional tuner or mechanic. The goal is to document the learning process from the beginning — including research, progress, problems and mistakes.

My current main project is my 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ. It's becoming my learning platform for understanding the mechanical and electronic sides of modern vehicles.
