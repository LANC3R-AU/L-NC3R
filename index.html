01 / ABOUT
L@NC3R

LEARNING • BUILDING • MODIFYING

WHO I AM

LEARNING CARS
ONE PROJECT AT A TIME.

I'm L@NC3R, a beginner automotive tuner and modder from Australia.

This portfolio documents my progression as I learn automotive diagnostics, ECU tuning, performance development, mechanical work and vehicle modification.

I'm not presenting myself as a professional tuner or mechanic. This is a record of the learning process from the beginning — including research, progress, problems, mistakes and discoveries.

My current main project is my 2009 Mitsubishi Lancer CJ. It is becoming my learning platform for understanding both the mechanical and electronic sides of a modern vehicle.

06 / DEVELOPMENT PATH

FROM STOCK
TO BUILT.

The long-term plan is to develop the car through knowledge and measured progress rather than changing parts without understanding why.

STAGE 01

Understand

Learn how the vehicle operates in its current configuration.

STAGE 02

Maintain

Understand the mechanical condition of the platform and develop maintenance skills.

STAGE 03

Diagnose

Learn to identify problems through evidence, diagnostic information and testing.

STAGE 04

Measure

Establish useful engine and vehicle baselines.

STAGE 05

Modify

Select modifications based on the goals of the build and what I've learned.

STAGE 06

Validate

Compare results against the previous baseline and document what actually changed.
